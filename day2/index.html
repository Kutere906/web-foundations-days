Our Manifesto

Built on Dedication.
Driven by Purpose.

As your KUPPET Tharaka School Welfare Representative, Ezra Kutere is committed to placing the dignity, wellbeing, and professional growth of every teacher at the heart of union action. This manifesto is a solemn covenant — a promise to serve with transparency, urgency, and unwavering resolve.

Improved Welfare Conditions

Who Is Ezra Kutere?

A Teacher Among Teachers

Ezra Kutere is a dedicated educator and committed union member who has walked the halls of Tharaka schools and understands firsthand the challenges that teachers face daily — from resource shortages to delayed welfare responses.

Grounded in community, guided by principle, and energised by a passion for service, Ezra brings both the empathy of a colleague and the drive of a proven advocate to this critical role.

Choosing Ezra means choosing someone who will show up, speak up, and follow through — because our schools and our teachers deserve nothing less.

10 Firm Commitments

What Ezra Will Do For You

  1. Establish a dedicated welfare desk with rapid response times for member grievances.
  2. Conduct quarterly school visits across Tharaka to hear issues directly from teachers.
  3. Push for resolution of all pending housing and accommodation welfare cases.
  4. Ensure transparent reporting on union funds and welfare expenditures.
  5. Collaborate with TSC on smooth and timely deployment and transfer processes.
  6. Organize legal aid clinics for members facing disciplinary or employment disputes.
  7. Champion affordable group medical cover expansion for KUPPET Tharaka members.
  8. Create a mentorship network linking senior and junior teachers across schools.
  9. Advocate for improved security and safety standards in all Tharaka schools.
  10. Produce a bi-monthly welfare newsletter keeping every member informed and engaged.
